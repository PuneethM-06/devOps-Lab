# Day 14 – IaaS, PaaS and SaaS Interview Questions

## Fundamentals

1. What is Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)?
2. What is Platform as a Service (PaaS)?
3. What is Software as a Service (SaaS)?
4. Give two examples of each service model.
5. Which model gives the most control and why?

---

## Shared Responsibility

6. Who patches the operating system in EC2?
7. Who manages the operating system in Google App Engine?
8. If your Java application crashes on EC2, whose responsibility is it?
9. If AWS has a physical server failure, whose responsibility is it?
10. In SaaS, what responsibilities do customers still have?

---

## Scenario Questions

11. Why would a startup prefer PaaS?
12. Why would a bank prefer IaaS?
13. Your application requires kernel tuning and custom drivers. Which service model would you choose?
14. Why can PaaS lead to vendor lock-in?
15. What happens if your PaaS provider experiences downtime?

---

## Advanced Questions

16. Is Amazon RDS closer to IaaS or PaaS? Why?
17. Is Kubernetes IaaS or PaaS?
18. How can containers reduce vendor lock-in?
19. Why is Docker more portable than a provider-specific service?
20. When would you choose SaaS over building your own solution?

---

## One-liner Questions

21. IaaS vs PaaS in one sentence.
22. PaaS vs SaaS in one sentence.
23. Why doesn't every company choose SaaS?
24. Which service model requires the most operational effort?
25. Which service model is best for rapid application development?
