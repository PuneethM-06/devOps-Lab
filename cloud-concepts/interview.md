# Day 13 – Cloud Fundamentals Interview Questions

## Fundamentals

1. What is cloud computing?
2. Why did companies traditionally build their own data centers?
3. What does "on-demand" mean in cloud computing?
4. Why is the statement "AWS is just someone else's computer" both correct and incomplete?
5. What is a Cloud Service Provider (CSP)?

## On-Prem vs Cloud

6. Explain three differences between on-prem and cloud.
7. Why is provisioning infrastructure faster in cloud?
8. Why would a startup prefer cloud over on-prem?
9. Why might a government organization prefer on-prem?
10. Is cloud always cheaper than on-prem? Explain.

## Pay-as-you-go

11. What does pay-as-you-go mean?

## Trade-offs

15. List three disadvantages of cloud.

## Vendor Lock-in

19. What is vendor lock-in?

## Scenario Questions

22. Your application gets 10x traffic overnight. Why is cloud beneficial?
